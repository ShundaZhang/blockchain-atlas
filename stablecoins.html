01 / FIELD NOTES

A stablecoin needs a peg mechanism beyond a token interface

Ask what can be redeemed, by whom, and against which assets.

DesignMechanismKey dependencies
Reserve-backedIssuer creates tokens against reserve/redemption arrangementsReserve assets, custody, issuer terms and liquidity
Crypto-collateralizedLocked collateral and liquidation rulesCollateral value, oracle freshness and liquidation capacity
Algorithmic / endogenousSupply changes or related-token incentives aim to hold a targetConfidence, reflexivity and available external backing

These categories can overlap. A quoted peg on an exchange is a market observation, not the same as a holder’s enforceable ability to redeem with an issuer. A token may trade away from its target even when its smart contract operates correctly.

02 / FIELD NOTES

USDT combines on-chain transfers with a centralized issuer

Decentralized settlement infrastructure does not remove issuer powers.

Tether publishes supported USD₮ protocols, reserve disclosures and service/redemption terms. Those records identify issuer-recognized deployments; the same USDT ticker on another contract is not enough. The on-chain token represents one layer, while reserves, banking, custody and redemption are off-chain arrangements.

Issuer-controlled token implementations can include privileged issuance and restrictions such as freezing/blacklisting under applicable mechanisms. Inspect the exact chain’s implementation and current terms. Reserve attestations are dated disclosures with a scope; they are not continuous on-chain verification of all reserve assets.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Chain, contract, units and transfer behavior all matter

Do not apply the STUDY example’s assumptions to every real token.

Ethereum USD₮ and TRON USD₮ live in different execution and fee environments; a transfer needs the relevant network’s fee asset. Check issuer-listed support and exact token contract before interpreting an address. Token decimals are deployment metadata, not universally 18; read the actual contract rather than copying the STUDY settings.

Some existing token contracts omit a standard boolean return or require a zero approval before a new nonzero approval. OpenZeppelin SafeERC20 includes compatibility helpers, but does not make an untrusted token safe in every sense. Read the implementation and test the integration.
