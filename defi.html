01 / FIELD NOTES

An AMM is a pricing rule over pooled reserves

A trade changes reserves and therefore changes the marginal price.

In a simplified constant-product pool, reserves x and y obey x × y = k. Input Δx obtains output Δy according to the pool formula, with fees modifying the effective input. Large trades relative to reserves move the price more. The interactive lab models a v2-style pool rather than concentrated-liquidity designs.

Price impact is the effect of your trade on the pool quote; slippage is the difference between expected and executed outcomes. A minimum-output bound can make an unfavorable execution revert. A current pool price is not automatically a trustworthy oracle for lending or settlement.

02 / FIELD NOTES

On-chain lending links collateral, debt and liquidation

Borrowing capacity is a risk rule, not free money.

A collateralized protocol tracks supplied assets, debt, oracle values and risk parameters. If debt grows or collateral value falls beyond permitted conditions, liquidation can repay debt against collateral under specified incentives. The mechanism depends on usable prices and execution liquidity.

Supply rates, borrow rates, utilization and liquidation thresholds are different quantities. A flash loan is borrowed and repaid within one transaction under its rules; it can amplify an oracle or logic weakness without by itself being a vulnerability. Inspect each market’s deployed parameters.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Composability also composes dependencies

Follow the asset and authority chain through the entire application.

A vault can deposit into a lending market, use a stablecoin and rely on an oracle or bridge. Each layer adds code, keys and liquidity assumptions. A displayed yield may combine variable borrowing demand, temporary rewards and asset-price changes; those should not be merged into one promised return.

Liquidity providers face changes in asset composition and value relative to holding the original assets, in addition to fee revenue. Governance or upgrade powers can alter strategy behavior. A useful diagram includes every dependency and exit path, not just the front-end brand.
